- A server you started yourself from a terminal shows up as what it is, and the panel notices when it stops instead of staying green over a process that is gone
- The Shared folder now follows the project of the tab you are on, instead of staying on the one belonging to the tab you came from

- While you are moving or copying files the mouse's back button walks you up through the folders to choose where they land, and Escape is what cancels, instead of the whole move being thrown away the moment you stepped back
