- The press effect is painted inside the element itself, so it takes its shape, stays within it and cannot be left lit on something you already let go of
- A conversation row tints whole, behind its text and its menu button, instead of only under the part you touched
- Inside a compressed file the path bar names the folders the way it does outside it, and stops at the project instead of climbing past it
